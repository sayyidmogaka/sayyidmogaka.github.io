Bio

I am an applied mathematician and computational researcher with an academic background in applied mathematics, informatics and petroleum engineering. My work and interests centre on mathematical modelling, scientific computing and computational approaches to subsurface systems.

I hold an MSc in Applied Mathematics and Informatics from the National Research Nuclear University MEPhI, where my research involved parallelising numerical solutions of heat-diffusion problems using OpenMPI and CUDA. I also hold a BSc in Petroleum Engineering from Gubkin Russian State University of Oil and Gas.

My current research interests include porous-media characterisation, geodesic tortuosity, multiphase transport, high-performance computing and machine learning for scientific applications.
