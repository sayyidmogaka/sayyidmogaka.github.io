Applied Mathematics & Scientific Computing

Computational Geoscience (Porous Media & Multiphase Flow) · Machine Learning

I work at the intersection of mathematical modelling, numerical computation and data-driven methods, with an interest in understanding complex physical processes in subsurface systems.

My background combines applied mathematics and informatics with petroleum engineering. I am particularly interested in computational approaches to porous-media characterisation, fluid transport and scientific machine learning.

Research interests

Applied mathematics. Partial differential equations, numerical analysis, mathematical modelling and computational methods for physical systems.

Scientific computing. High-performance and parallel computing, numerical algorithms, simulation and reproducible workflows.

Computational geoscience. Porous-media characterisation, geodesic tortuosity, multiphase flow and subsurface transport.

Machine learning. Data-driven modelling and scientific machine learning that complement physics-based simulation.
